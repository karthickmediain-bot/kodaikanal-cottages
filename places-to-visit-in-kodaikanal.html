Notable Places to Visit in Kodaikanal

Ten attractions that cover the lake, the viewpoints, the falls and the gardens.

Lake

Kodaikanal Lake

A star-shaped man-made lake, ideal for boating, cycling and leisurely strolls around its scenic perimeter.

Best forBoating & cycling
Viewpoint

Coaker’s Walk

A scenic pathway with stunning views of the mountains and valleys, especially beautiful at sunrise and sunset.

Best forSunrise & sunset
ViewpointD

Dolphin’s Nose

A popular viewpoint with breathtaking panoramic views of the hills and valleys.

Best forPanoramic views
Lake

Berijam Lake

A serene, less-crowded lake that is ideal for picnics and nature walks. Entry needs a forest permit.

Best forPicnics & walks
WaterfallP

Pambar Falls

Also known as Fairy Falls, perfect for picnics and photography amid lush surroundings.

Best forPicnics & photos
Waterfall

Silver Cascade Falls

A stunning waterfall on the way up to Kodaikanal, known for its beauty and refreshing water.

Best forA first stop
Garden

Bryant Park

A botanical garden with a vast collection of flowers and plants, offering a peaceful ambience.

Best forFlowers & calm
ScienceK

Kodaikanal Solar Observatory

A place for astronomy enthusiasts, with insights into solar studies and panoramic views.

Best forAstronomy fans
Park

Chettiar Park

A well-maintained park that is ideal for family outings and easy walks.

Best forFamily outings
Adventure

Guna Cave

Also known as Devil’s Kitchen, a mysterious and adventurous exploration experience.

Best forExploring
