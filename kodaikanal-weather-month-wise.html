Climate

Kodaikanal Climate at a Glance

Kodaikanal is mostly visited for its refreshing climate. It is tropical (classified Am by the Köppen-Geiger system), so most months see significant rainfall and the short dry season has very little impact.

Best time to visit: April & May

Pleasant, green and sunny, with the warmest days topping out around 22 °C.

FAQ

Kodaikanal Weather FAQ

Quick answers before you pack.

What is the best time to visit Kodaikanal?

April and May. The summers are pleasant, green and sunny, with highs of up to 22 °C.

How cold does Kodaikanal get in winter?

Winter is chilly and foggy, between 5 °C and 17 °C. It can sometimes drop to freezing point.

When does it rain most?

Heavy rain falls from June to September. The greatest rainfall is in October, and May has the most rainy days.

Which month is the sunniest?

March, with an average of 9.52 hours of sunshine a day. January is the least sunny, at 5.48 hours.
