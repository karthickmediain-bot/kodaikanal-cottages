Climate

Kodaikanal Climate at a Glance

Cool air is the reason most people come. At about 2,100 m, the town stays mild while the plains below swelter. Rain falls in most months, and the short dry spell does little to change that.

Best months: April and May

Pleasant, green and sunny, with the warmest days around 22 °C.

Conditions

Kodaikanal Rain, Fog and Frost

Rain

About 2,630 mm falls through the year, so carry a raincoat from June to October.

Mist and fog

Mist peaks at dawn and dusk and sits thickest on lake and viewpoint roads. Plan sightseeing for mid-morning.

Frost and snowfall

There is no snowfall, but frost can form on the coldest nights in December and January.

Roads

Ghat roads slow down in heavy rain and fog. Start early, avoid night driving and check road status first.

Waterfalls

Stays

FAQ

Kodaikanal Weather FAQ

Quick answers before you pack.

What is the weather in Kodaikanal today?

The live widget at the top of this page shows the current reading and the next 15 days. Mornings are usually cool and misty, afternoons mild, and evenings cool quickly, so keep a jacket handy.

What is the best time to visit Kodaikanal?

April and May suit most travellers, with green hills, sunshine and highs near 22 °C. December and January work if you enjoy cold mornings and cosy stays. Book either window three to four weeks ahead.

Is Kodaikanal cold in December and January?

Yes. Kodaikanal weather in December brings nights near 5 °C and fog until mid-morning. Days are clearer and calmer, so layers work better than one heavy coat. A heater in your room helps.

Does it snow in Kodaikanal?

There is no snowfall. Frost can form on the coldest December and January nights, and mist is common all year, which sometimes makes mornings look wintry. Pack woollens and expect a damp chill rather than snow.

Which months have the most rain in Kodaikanal?

Heavy rain falls from June to September. October records the greatest rainfall, and May has the most rainy days. January is the driest month, so it suits travellers who want clear viewpoints.

What should I wear in Kodaikanal?

Choose heavy woollens, a cap and gloves from December to February. Light layers with a jacket suit March to May. From June to October, add a raincoat and shoes with good grip.

Is Kodaikanal safe in the monsoon?

Yes, with care. Roads run slowly in heavy rain and fog, so travel in daylight and check conditions first. Cottages Kodaikanal can help with cabs and rooms that suit the monsoon season.
