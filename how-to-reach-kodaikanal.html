Key takeaway

From Bangalore

Bangalore to Kodaikanal: Road, Bus, Train and Flight

About 460 km and 8 to 10 hours. Four options cover how to reach Kodaikanal from Bangalore, and most visitors leave overnight to arrive for breakfast.

01

By car or cab

Take NH 44 south to Dindigul, then climb through the Palani Hills. The highway stays four lanes for most of the way, and a cab leaves when you do. You reach your stay without changing vehicle, and the driver handles the hairpins.

02

By bus

Overnight buses run from Bangalore to the hill town's main bus stop. Fares and timings change by season, so confirm both with the operator. Arriving at dawn leaves you a full day.

03

By train

Take an overnight train to Dindigul or Madurai and book it on IRCTC. A cab covers the remaining 100 to 120 km, so arrange it before you leave.

04

By air

Fly from Bangalore to Madurai, then take a cab for about three hours. This suits a short weekend, when a full night on the road would eat into your time.

From Chennai

Chennai to Kodaikanal: Train, Bus and Road Options

About 500 km and 9 to 11 hours by road. Here is how to reach Kodaikanal from Chennai without losing a day.

Cab from Chennai
01

By train, then cab

Overnight trains from Chennai to Madurai stop at Kodai Road, 80 km from the hill town. You arrive in the morning, and the cab takes you straight up. It is the most relaxed option for families.

02

By car or cab

The usual route runs through Trichy and Dindigul on the national highway. Leave in the early evening and you arrive the next morning, rested enough to enjoy day one.

03

By bus

State and private overnight buses run between Chennai and the hills. Timings shift with the season, so check with the operator before you book.

04

By air

Fly Chennai to Madurai, then take a cab for the last 120 km. The flight removes the overnight leg.

By bus

Kodaikanal by Bus: Bus Stand and Cities Covered

Buses connect the hill town to the main cities in Tamil Nadu and beyond.

01

Where the bus drops you

Buses stop at the Kodaikanal bus stand in the middle of town, a short ride from most stays. From there it is a quick cab or an uphill walk, so keep luggage light.

02

Which cities have buses

Regular services run from Madurai, Dindigul, Palani, Coimbatore, Chennai and Bangalore. Nearby towns have buses through the day, while long-distance services mostly leave in the evening.

03

When a cab is easier

A cab works better for families, heavy luggage, late arrivals and groups of four or more. It picks you up where you choose and takes you directly to your stay.

The climb

The Kodaikanal Ghat Road: What to Expect

Most of the journey is easy highway. The last 30 km or so climbs through hairpin bends to about 2,100 metres, as noted in Wikipedia's Kodaikanal entry.

01

Travel in daylight

Fog and mist cut visibility on the bends, especially after sunset. Arrive before dark, or travel with a driver who knows the road.

02

Take it slow

Allow extra time for the climb. Heavy rain makes the surface slippery, and slow trucks are common on the bends.

03

Carry motion-sickness tablets

The turns affect many passengers, especially children. Sit in front and skip heavy meals before the climb.

04

Self-drive or hire a driver

Self-driving is possible, but a local driver is calmer on the bends and the descent. See our car rental for both options.

FAQ

How to Reach Kodaikanal: FAQ

Quick answers for planning your arrival.

Which is the nearest railway station to Kodaikanal?

Kodai Road is the Kodaikanal nearest railway station, about 80 km and 2.5 hours by road. Palani, Dindigul and Madurai Junction also work. Pre-book a cab so a driver meets your train.

Which is the nearest airport to Kodaikanal?

Madurai Airport is nearest, about 120 km and three hours away. The Madurai airport to Kodaikanal drive suits most flights from Chennai, Bangalore and Hyderabad. Coimbatore Airport, around 180 km away, is the second option.

Is there a train to Kodaikanal?

No. Kodaikanal by train means rail to Kodai Road, Palani, Dindigul or Madurai, then a road climb of 65 to 120 km. A pre-booked cab makes the second leg simple.

How do I reach Kodaikanal from Bangalore?

The Bangalore to Kodaikanal distance is about 460 km, or 8 to 10 hours. Drive or take a cab overnight, board a bus, or ride an overnight train to Dindigul and continue by cab.

How do I reach Kodaikanal from Chennai?

The Chennai to Kodaikanal distance is about 500 km, or 9 to 11 hours by road. Most visitors take an overnight train to Kodai Road, Dindigul or Madurai, then a cab. Driving, buses and flights to Madurai also work.

Is the ghat road to Kodaikanal safe to drive?

Yes, with care. The Kodaikanal ghat road has hairpin bends over its last 30 km or so and can turn foggy or slippery. Travel in daylight, drive slowly, or hire a local driver.

Where does the bus to Kodaikanal stop?

Buses stop at the Kodaikanal bus stand in the middle of town, a short ride from most stays. Cottages Kodaikanal can arrange a cab from there if you message us your arrival time.
