How to Reach Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal has no railway station or airport of its own. You reach the hills by road from a nearby city or station, and the last 20 km is a winding ghat climb.

Getting here

Three Ways to Get to Kodaikanal

Whichever way you travel, the final stretch is by road.

Every step of the way

Most of the journey is easy highway. The last 20 km climbs through hairpin bends to about 2,100 metres, so allow extra time and travel in daylight if you can.

By air

Madurai Airport is the nearest, about 120 km and roughly 3 hours away. Coimbatore Airport is the other main option, at around 180 km and 4 to 5 hours. Pre-book a cab so a driver is waiting when you land.

By train

Kodai Road is the nearest station, about 80 km and roughly 2.5 hours away. Palani (65 km), Dindigul (100 km) and Madurai Junction (120 km) are the other common arrival points. Take a cab or bus for the rest.

By road

The most flexible option. Private cabs run door to door from Madurai, Coimbatore, Dindigul, Palani and Kodai Road, and state-transport buses connect the same towns to Kodaikanal bus stand. Self-driving is possible, but many visitors prefer a driver who knows the hairpin bends.

FAQ

How to Reach Kodaikanal: FAQ

Quick answers for planning your arrival.

Which is the nearest railway station to Kodaikanal?

Kodai Road, about 80 km and roughly 2.5 hours by road.

Which is the nearest airport to Kodaikanal?

Madurai Airport, about 120 km and roughly 3 hours by road. Coimbatore Airport is around 180 km away.

Is there a train to Kodaikanal?

No. Take a train to Kodai Road, Palani, Dindigul or Madurai and continue by cab or bus.
