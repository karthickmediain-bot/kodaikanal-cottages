## Key finding

Two things about this page:

1. **The exact phrase "kodaikanal 3 day tour package" has no volume**, and the near twin "kodaikanal 3 days tour package" is only 10. The usable head term is **"kodaikanal tour packages for 3 days" at 320** (Medium competition, index 36), the plural/"for" form people actually type.
2. **The itinerary intent is as large as the package intent.** "kodaikanal 3 day itinerary" and "kodaikanal 3 days itinerary" are 210 each, and "trip plan for 3 days" is 110. People planning 3 days look for an itinerary first, so the day-by-day plan must be on this page.

Demand is seasonal: the 320 head term reaches 590 in April and May, and drops to 170 in July. Refresh before March.

Compared with the 2 day page, this one has less total demand (320 vs 590 for the head term, and about 340 for 3-day places vs 2,200 for 2-day). Most of the uplift is in the itinerary phrases, not the places lists.

This page is **2 nights 3 days**. The 3 nights 4 days page (`3-nights-4-days-couple-tour-package-kodaikanal.md`) owns the 4 day phrasings. Do not mix them.

Do not target "kodaikanal travel package / trip package / holiday packages for kodaikanal" (3,600 each). Those belong to the tour packages hub.

### Recommended changes to the structure above

1. **Rewrite H2 #9:** "Kodaikanal 3 Day Package from Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad and Madurai" (carrying 720, 590, 90 and 70).
2. **Add a three-tier price table:** budget, mid-range, premium, with per-person and per-vehicle rows.
3. **Add a hotel block** (star rating, room type, location) and a **meal plan** row.
4. **Add "What is not included":** entry fees, jeep safari and adventure activities, toy train, extra meals, tips.
5. **Add a Day 3 leisure and shopping H3** (the competitor's Day 3 is shopping and departure).
6. **Add a family variation H3** (leisure day, kid-friendly stops) next to the couples and honeymoon H3.
7. **Add a terms block:** advance, installments, cancellation, customisation.
8. **Add a trust line and reviews.**
9. **Add three FAQs:** "What is not included?", "How much advance do I pay?", "Can the itinerary be customised?"

The gap to use: the competitor reviews show the common failures are skipped stops, hotel cleanliness and breakfast timing. A page that promises the full stop list, the hotel standard and breakfast timing answers what the reviews complain about.
