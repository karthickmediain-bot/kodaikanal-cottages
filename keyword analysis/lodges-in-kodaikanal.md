How they work:
- "Yagappa lodge" and "hotel Yagappa" return the same volume because they are one property searched two ways. Many people looking for a named lodge use the word "lodge" loosely for a hotel.
- Yagappa and GRT also appear in the Resorts page brand list, so they are shared across pages.

How to use them without targeting them:
- Capture your own brand: put "Cottages Kodaikanal lodge" in the title and schema.
- Don't name competitors on the page.
- Match the comparison attributes searchers use: location (bus stand, lake), price, booking.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Aggregators: CozyCozy, Expedia, Holidify, Orbitz, Travelocity.
- Travel guide: Adventure Collective ("Kodaikanal Adventure Basecamp Guide").
- Weak relevance: Travelocity's Dindigul District page and Expedia's Andipatti page also rank, which means the local competition for "lodges in kodaikanal" is thin.

What they push:
- Volume: one listing shows 432 lodges with filters for price, type and rating.
- Location advice: lodges near the lake and main road suit boats and transport; ridge-top lodges give quieter nights and earlier sunrise views.
- Family rooms, garden or mountain views, on-site restaurants (The Pear County).
- Temple-area and budget options (Holidify).

Your page against that:
- Already has H2s for featured lodges, booking, the area, tour packages, reviews, planning and FAQ.
- Gap (volume): no H2 uses the "low price" or "cheap" wording (90 / 20).
- Gap: no H2 on location. "Near bus stand" (70 + 20 + 20) and "near lake" (20) are the lodge-specific location searches.
- Gap: no heading with "price" or "tariff" (20 each).
- Gap: no guest-house, cabin or Vattakanal angle (30 / 10).
- The H1 ("A Kodaikanal Lodge, Not Just a Room With a View") is good copy but does not contain the exact phrase "lodges in kodaikanal". Consider adding it.
- Location advice is an opening: competitors give generic tips, and you can give specific walking distances to the bus stand and lake.

Cannibalization watch:
- Hotels page: owns every "hotel" term and probably the "places to stay" hub.
- Dormitory page: owns "budget places to stay" and "cheap places to stay".
- Resorts page: also lists Yagappa and GRT as brands.
- "cheap lodges in kodaikanal" (20) is the only cheap-wording term here. Keep it to lodge phrasing.

Caveat: the lodge niche totals about 900 searches a month. Expect this page to rank for the lodge terms with little effort (the SERP has weak local results), but traffic will be modest. Treat it as a conversion page for visitors who arrive from other pages.
