How they work:

How to use them without targeting them:
- Capture your own brand: put "Cottages Kodaikanal villas" in the title and schema.
- Don't name competitors on the page.
- Match what name-searchers compare (bedrooms, pool, view, price) in the generic H2s above.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Villa specialists: StayVista (multi-BHK villas), Amã Stays & Trails (Kailash House, Kumara Villa, Sneh Villa).
- Aggregators: Expedia, Travelocity, Airbnb, Trip.com, Only-Apartments.
- Individual listings: Silver Mist (5 bedrooms, near the lake), Tree Top Villa (7 bedrooms), Le Hayat, Skysketch Villa.

What they push:
- Bedroom count and BHK in the title.
- Private pools, valley views, gardens, breakfast included.
- Price ranges: budget villas about $35–80 a night, mid to luxury about $90–460.
- Sold-out dates and live availability.

Your page against that:
- The H1 is clear ("Villas in Kodaikanal, a Full House for Your Family").
- Already has H2s for featured villas, booking, the area, tour packages, reviews, planning and FAQ.
- Gap (high volume): the word "villa stay" (880) appears only in "Planning Your Kodaikanal Villa Stay".
- Gap: no H2 for private or luxury villas (140 / 90).
- Gap: no H2 for pool villas (90 + 70 + 70 + 50 across variants).
- Gap: no H2 for group villa stays (110).
- Gap: no "for rent" or price wording near the top.
- Gap: "farm villa" (70) and "lake villa" (30) are unaddressed angles.

Cannibalization watch:
- "villa for sale in kodaikanal" (140) and "luxury villas for sale" (10) are buy intent. Skip them.
- Pool terms must stay villa-framed. "Resorts with private pool" (720) is for the Resorts page.
- "3 bedroom villa in kodaikanal" (30) touches the home page's bedroom-cottage section. Keep it body-copy only and use "villa" wording.

Caveat: a single brand ("Villa Retreat") owns the biggest villa searches, and aggregators will beat a single-property page on the head terms. Best targets are the modifiers: stay, private, luxury, pool, booking, group.
