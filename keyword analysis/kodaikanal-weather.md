# Keyword Analysis: "Kodaikanal Weather"

Page: Kodaikanal weather (weather, temperature, climate and best time to visit guide)
Date: 2026-10-29
Status: draft, awaiting approval.
Source: Google Ads Keyword Planner (India), monthly average searches (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026). Runs: keyword ideas from 4 seeds (115 ideas) and a 30-phrase check of live-weather, month-wise, season and planning phrases.
Brand terms excluded. "No data" means Keyword Planner returned no volume, not that the phrase was skipped.

## Key finding

"Kodaikanal weather" is the largest keyword in this project: **135,000 a month**, competition index 0, and the whole cluster is well over 300,000. The intent splits in two, and the page has to serve both:

1. **Live-conditions queries** (about 200,000): weather 135,000, temperature 33,100, "temp" 33,100, weather today 27,100, temperature now 14,800, climate now 8,100, weather now 5,400, weather tomorrow 1,000. People want a number right now. A written guide will not satisfy them. Put a **live forecast widget (current temperature, 7 to 15 day forecast) at the top of the page** or Google will keep serving weather tools and big weather sites.
2. **Planning queries** (about 40,000): best time to visit and best season (5,400 each phrasing), climate 2,900, month-wise weather (December 720, May 1,600, April 390, June 390, July 390). This is where a travel business wins, and the written content below the widget serves it.

Seasonality is strong. Live-weather queries peak in April (246,000 for the head term) and December (246,000), and drop to 60,500 in February. Month queries rise a month or two ahead of the month itself (for example "weather in May" peaks at 8,100 in April). Publish or refresh month sections two months ahead.

The earlier tour-package analysis called weather "a tool query, not worth chasing" for the packages hub. That still holds for the hub. This dedicated page is where the weather demand belongs, and its job is to hand planning traffic to the tour and stay pages.

### Recommended changes to the structure above

1. **Add a verdict box** under the live widget: best months, months to avoid, one-line reason each.
2. **Add an H2: "Kodaikanal Events and Festivals by Month"** (flower show and summer festival in May, Pongal in January, Panguni Uthiram in March).
3. **Add an H2: "Kodaikanal Monsoon Travel Guide"** (rain, landslide-prone sections, waterfalls, off-season stay prices).
4. **Give each month block the same five rows:** temperature, rain, fog, crowd level, what to do. Add a stay link (cottages, packages) at the end of each block.
5. **Add a month comparison table** (best, crowds, rain, price).
6. **Add a quick-facts block:** altitude (1,300), sunrise and sunset, air quality, humidity.
7. **Add three FAQs:** "What is the altitude of Kodaikanal?", "Is Kodaikanal safe in the monsoon?", "When is the Kodaikanal flower show?"

None of the competitors cover everything. The strongest gap is **stay and booking advice per season**: the travel sites that rank (MakeMyTrip, Veena World, StayVista) sell packages or villas, and TrekGo only links to related articles. This site can give month-specific stay advice with real cottage availability, which is the conversion the weather page exists for.

## Cannibalization note

This page owns weather, temperature, climate and month-wise phrasings. Keep it distinct from:
- the tour packages hub, which owns "best time to visit" only as a short planning block (2,400 on its H2 list). Make that block a summary that links here, and use the full "best time to visit" H2 on this page;
- the trip and places pages, which own "places to visit" and "things to do";
- the stay pages (cottages, hotels, villas), which should link here from a "good time to stay" note.

Do not repeat month blocks on other pages. Link to this page from every package and stay page, and link from this page to the 1, 2 and 3 day packages, the cottage pages and the car rental page.

## Content risk

Live temperature figures go stale quickly. Use an embedded forecast widget or a regularly refreshed data source for the live block. Write the month sections with ranges and descriptions, not fixed daily numbers, and have someone check the ranges against a weather source before publishing.
