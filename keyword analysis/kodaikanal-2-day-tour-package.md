## Key finding

The exact phrase "kodaikanal 2 day tour package" has **no volume of its own**, but Keyword Planner groups it with **"kodaikanal 2 days tour package" and "kodaikanal tour packages for 2 days" at 590 a month**. Use "2 days tour package" as the working primary (590, Low competition, index 32).

Compared with the 1 day page this one has real demand:
- Package head term: 590 (the 1 day equivalent is 90).
- Places for 2 days: 1,300 for "tourist places in kodaikanal for 2 days", plus 480 + 480 for the "places to visit / see in 2 days" phrasings.
- Planning: "trip plan for 2 days" 260 and "itinerary for kodaikanal" 590.

Demand peaks in April and May (about 880 for the head term and 590 for places), so refresh the page before March.

Do not target "holiday packages for kodaikanal" or "kodaikanal travel package / trip package" (3,600 each). Those belong to the tour packages hub.

### Recommended changes to the structure above

1. **Rewrite H2 #9:** "Kodaikanal 2 Day Package from Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad and Madurai" (carrying 720, 590, 90 and 70).
2. **Add a price table:** per person (twin sharing) and per vehicle, with hotel level.
3. **Add a hotel block:** star rating, room type, location.
4. **Add "What is not included":** entry fees, jeep safari, boating, extra meals, tips.
5. **Add a meal plan row** (breakfast, dinner) to inclusions.
6. **Add a terms block:** advance, installments, cancellation, customisation.
7. **Add a trust line and reviews** ("travel agency" wording, 390).
8. **Add three FAQs:** "What is not included?", "How much advance do I pay?", "Is pickup from the bus stand or railway station included?"

The gap to use: competitors' reviews show the failures are skipped itinerary stops and hotel cleanliness. A page that states the fixed route, hotel standard and driver rules answers the fears that reviews raise.
