How they work:
- Brand searches dwarf the generic ones. Many of the biggest "resort" keywords are people looking for a specific named property.
- Most follow the pattern `[brand] + resort/hotel + kodaikanal`.

How to use them without targeting them:
- Capture your own brand: put "Cottages Kodaikanal resorts" in the title and schema.
- Don't name competitors on the page.
- Cover the shared attributes searchers use to compare brands (pool, lake view, family, price). The generic H2s above do that.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Listicles and blogs: Housing.com "top 10 resorts in Kodaikanal", Veena World blog, several travel blogs.
- Aggregators: Hotels.com, Kayak, Expedia (Expedia lists 59 resorts, roughly $26–$214).

What they push:
- Named-property lists with star ratings and price.
- Lake distance, spa, golf, pool, cottages and tents.
- Couple, family and romantic groupings.

Your page against that:
- Already has H2s for family, booking, the area around the resort, tour packages, reviews, events and FAQ.
- Gap (high volume): no H2 for pool resorts (1,600).
- Gap (high volume): nothing for couples (480).
- Gap (high volume): nothing for luxury or 5-star (590 / 390).
- Gap (high volume): nothing for Vattakanal (480).
- Gap: "best resorts in kodaikanal" (2,900) is not in any H2.
- The H1 ("Resorts in Kodaikanal for Family Stays and Big Events") is fine. Consider adding "in Kodai" to the intro to catch the 22,200 variant.

Cannibalization watch:
- "hotels kodaikanal" (18,100), "good hotels in kodaikanal" (1,900) and "5 star hotels in kodaikanal" (1,600) belong on the Hotels page.
- "lodges in kodaikanal" (210) belongs on the Lodges page.
- "villa stay in kodaikanal" (880) belongs on the Villas page.
- The "hotels & resorts in kodaikanal" keyword (14,800) overlaps with Hotels. Keep it to one page; recommended: use it here only as a secondary phrase.

Caveat: aggregators and big brands will beat a single-property page on the head terms. Best targets are the modifiers: family, pool, couples, lake, Vattakanal, booking.
