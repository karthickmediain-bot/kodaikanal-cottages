## How the bus, train and station keywords are used

As on the Coimbatore and Kodai Road pages, you do not sell bus or train tickets, so these are **bridge keywords**: the visitor searches for a bus or train, the page answers briefly, and the car is the call to action.

Rules for this page:
- Never promise bus or train bookings. Say what the options are, then "or go door to door by car".
- Give third-party figures (bus fare, timings, distance) as "about", and check them before publishing. They change.
- One source says TNSTC tickets for this route are sold only at the Dindigul bus stand counter. That is unverified; do not repeat it as fact.
- Dindigul has a railway junction, and the page already has "Is Dindigul Railway Station Pickup Available?". Keep that as the car pickup anchor.

## Key finding

This route has the **second largest informational demand** after Kodai Road. "Dindigul to Kodaikanal distance" is **8,100** a month, the plain route term is 3,600, and bus searches add about 3,600 more. Cab and taxi searches for the route total about 1,100, which is better than the other route pages.

The route also offers a real choice. A source gives about 75 km via Batlagundu (about 2.5 to 3 hours) and about 100 km via Palani. A page that explains the two roads honestly, then offers a driver who knows them, answers the question the distance searchers are asking.

What they push (figures from the results; they differ by source):
- Distance: about 75 km via Batlagundu, about 100 km via Palani; other sources say 92 to 95 km. Time about 2.3 to 3 hours.
- Route detail: the Batlagundu road is wider and better maintained; the Laws Ghat road connects the route.
- Buses: regular TNSTC services from the Dindigul bus stand, plus many private operators on aggregators.

Your page against that:
- The title ("Dindigul to Kodaikanal Cab | Fare, Route & Booking") matches the cab-fare intent.

- The page opens with "How Can You Travel from Dindigul to Kodaikanal?" and covers cost, route choice, station pickup, tour package, best time, booking, reviews and FAQs.
- Gap (volume): no heading says "distance" (8,100).
- Gap: no heading carries bus (880 + 880 + 170 + 140 + 140 + 140) or the reverse direction "kodaikanal to dindigul" (390).
- Gap: no heading says "taxi" or "cab" in the plain route form (320 each), apart from the cost and booking headings.
- Strong existing material: "Which Route Should You Take from Dindigul to Kodaikanal?" and "Is Dindigul Railway Station Pickup Available?" are real differentiators. Make the route heading carry the distance: "Dindigul to Kodaikanal Distance: Via Batlagundu or Via Palani".
