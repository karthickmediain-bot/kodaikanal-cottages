## Key finding

"Cab" and "taxi" are the same product to searchers, but the volumes are split: the taxi terms are larger (3,600 for taxi service against 1,000 for cab service). The cab pool is healthy for a service page (about 880 to 1,000 for the head terms, 8 keywords above 100), and the search results contain **no local operator**: only outstation booking platforms (HurryUp Cabs, MakeMyTrip). A local sightseeing and transfer cab service has an open field.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Outstation platforms: HurryUp Cabs (Madurai to Kodaikanal, Bangalore to Kodaikanal) and MakeMyTrip route pages (Kodaikanal to Dindigul, Tirur, Namakkal, Delhi, Nellore, Kolar).
- No local Kodaikanal operator, and nothing for local sightseeing or airport pickup.

What they push:
- Route landing pages with distance and one-way fares (for example Kodaikanal to Tirur about 268 km from INR 6,480; Namakkal from INR 5,077; Dindigul about 95 km; Madurai about 118 km).
- Vehicle choices: hatchback, sedan, SUV, tempo traveller.
- Payment options (UPI, cards, wallets), customer ratings, ability to propose your own fare.

Your page against that:
- The H1 ("Cab Service in Kodaikanal") matches the 1,000-volume phrase.
- The page covers what the service is, types, what you can book it for, cost, where it can take you, families, inclusions, best time, how to book and FAQs. The headings are question-style.
- Gap (volume): no heading uses the plain "cabs" phrasing (880) or "cab in kodaikanal" (590).
- Gap: no heading for sightseeing cabs (390 + 40 + 20 + 20).
- Gap: "How Can You Book a Cab in Kodaikanal?" is the closest to cab booking (210). Add "cab booking" wording to the heading.
- Gap: no heading for "best cab service" (170), "cab package" (140), or "cab rental" (110 each).
- Gap: no airport pickup keyword. The title promises "Airport Pickup", but the keyword tool returned no volume for it, so treat it as a service feature, not an SEO target.
- Easy renames:
  - "What Types of Cab Service Are Available in Kodaikanal?" to "Cab Packages in Kodaikanal: Sightseeing, Transfers and Airport Pickup"
  - "What Can You Book a Kodaikanal Cab For?" to "Cabs in Kodaikanal for Sightseeing"
  - "How Can You Book a Cab in Kodaikanal?" to "Kodaikanal Cab Booking: How It Works"
  - Add "Best Cab Service in Kodaikanal" as the heading above the "Why choose us" content
- Local detail is the opening: fixed local sightseeing packages, ghat-road experienced drivers, pickup from your cottage or hotel.

Cannibalization watch:
- Call Taxi page: the biggest risk. "Cab service" (1,000) and "taxi service" (3,600) are the same product. Keep "taxi" wording off this page, and keep "pre-booked sightseeing and transfers" here against "local on-call" there.
- Car Rental and Car Hire pages: keep "car rental" and "car hire" wording off this page. This page keeps "cab rental" and "cabs rental".
- Route pages: link to them for the city fare pages and do not duplicate them.

Caveat: cab searches are modest in absolute terms (about 880 to 1,000 for the head phrase), but a local operator can win them because the results are all outstation platforms. Treat this page as a conversion page for visitors who arrive from stays, tours and routes.
