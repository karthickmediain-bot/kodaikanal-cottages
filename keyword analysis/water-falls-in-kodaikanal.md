## Facts from research (verify before publishing)

- Silver Cascade is about 8 km from town. Sources say entry is free but disagree on hours (6 to 6, 9:30 to 3, or 24 hours). The page says "confirm on the day".
- Bear Shola is about 2 km from Kodai Lake, about 40 ft high. Hours and fees vary by source (free, or about Rs 5).
- Pambar Falls is also called Liril Falls and Grand Cascade.
- Vattakanal is a short trek from Pambar House, with Anjuveedu as a quieter twin fall nearby.
- Silver Cascade height is quoted as 180 ft by some sources and 54 m by others.
