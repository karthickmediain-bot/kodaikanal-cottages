"Dormitory" is a small term (390/mo). Searchers say **"hostel"** instead: "hostel in kodaikanal", "kodaikanal hostels" and "hostels in kodai" are 1,600 each. The page should use both words. How they work:
- Hostel brand searches (about 8,000/mo combined) are much bigger than "dormitory in kodaikanal" (390). People looking for a dorm bed often search by brand.
- Most follow the pattern `[brand] + kodaikanal`.

How to use them without targeting them:
- Compete on the shared comparison attributes: per-bed price, mixed or female dorm, lockers, Wi-Fi, location. These show up in the generic H2s above.
- Don't name competitors on the page.
- Capture your own brand: put "Cottages Kodaikanal dormitory" in the title and schema.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Hostel platforms and aggregators: Hostelworld, Hostelz, Hotels.com, Expedia, LBB.
- Hostel brands: Zostel Kodaikanal, The Hosteller Kodaikanal.
- Noise: Kodaikanal International School dormitory history (Wikipedia).

What they push:
- Per-bed pricing. Zostel shows about INR 499 per bed (8-bed mixed dorm) and INR 599 (6-bed female dorm). Hostelz shows beds from about $2 and an average of about $9.
- Mixed and female-only dorms, lockers, Wi-Fi, shared washrooms.
- The Hosteller: 4- and 6-bed A/C dorms with private bathrooms and valley views.

Your page against that:
- Already has H2s for options, booking, area, budget tours, reviews, planning and FAQ.
- Gap (high volume): the word "hostel" appears in no H2 except by implication in the H1 ("Minus the Hostel Guesswork"). That is the 1,600-volume term.
- Gap (high volume): no H2 for budget or cheap places to stay (880 / 320).
- Gap (high volume): nothing for youth hostel (170).
- Gap: no per-bed price in a heading. Competitors lead with it.
- Gap (low volume): Vattakanal hostels (20 each) are not covered.
- A group angle is your edge: most competitors are backpacker hostels, while you can pitch dorm stays for student groups, trekking groups and tour parties.

Cannibalization watch:
- "budget stay in kodaikanal" (880) was used as an H2 on the Resorts page. This page uses "budget places to stay in kodaikanal" (same volume) instead to avoid overlap.
- "cheapest hotels in kodaikanal" (320) may overlap with the Hotels page. Use as a secondary phrase only.

Spam flag:
- The search for "dormitory in kodaikanal" returned a Russian Laravel forum thread promoting "Cottages Kodaikanal". It looks like link spam. Worth checking your backlinks in Search Console and disavowing if the domain is junk.

Caveat: Zostel and The Hosteller own the brand searches. The best targets are the generic hostel and budget terms, plus group-stay angles.
