## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Travel-listing sites: TourTravelWorld ("Kodaikanal Tent Stay" and "Adventures Stay" packages), Trip.com ("Kodaikanal Tent Stay Jeep Ride"), anar.biz.
- Accommodation aggregators: bedandbreakfast.eu listings (BumpyTrailsCabin, Divercity Luxury Glamp, Croods Cabin).
- Few strong operator sites appear, which suggests the niche is under-served.

What they push:
- 24-hour jeep-safari camping packages: 4x4 ride, waterfall trek, sunset hike, bonfire, dinner, stargazing, sunrise walk. One listing shows about INR 1,800 per adult.
- Inclusions: 2-person tents with mattresses and sleeping bags, three meals, BBQ.
- Luxury glamping: outdoor fireplace, spa, wellness packages.

Your page against that:
- The H1 already includes the campfire hook ("With a Real Campfire Every Night").
- Already has H2s for featured tent sites, booking, the area, tour packages, reviews and FAQ.
- Gap: the word "camp" or "camping" appears in no H2 except "Camp Night". That covers 140, 140, 110 and 90-volume terms.
- Gap: no H2 for a jeep ride or jeep-camp combo (70), which is the format competitors lead with.
- Gap: no H2 for campfire (40 + 40 + 30).
- Gap: no coverage of luxury, glamping or dome tents (long-tail).
- Gap: no price or inclusions block near the top. Competitors list a price and what is included.
- Cross-sell: you already sell jeep safaris, so a jeep-and-camp package page or section is a natural fit.

Cannibalization watch:
- "kodaikanal resorts with campfire" (20) and "pet friendly resorts" belong on the Resorts page. This page uses only the tent angle.
- "kodaikanal cottage and tent stay" (0) has no volume, but avoid it as a heading since it overlaps the Villas and Cottages pages.

Caveat: the whole niche is roughly 1,500 searches a month in total. Expect modest traffic from SEO, so treat this page mainly as a conversion page for people who reach it from tours, jeep safari and the home page.
