## Scope rule: no clash with the home page

The home page (`index.html`) owns "Cottages in Kodaikanal" in its title, H1, FAQ and several H2s. It also owns:
- 2, 3, 4 and 5 bedroom cottages
- Vattakanal and lakeside cottage locations
- budget / lowest-price cottages
- luxury and private cottages
- cottage booking

This page must not target any of those. Only keywords that include a **group** intent are kept here. That removes "cottages in kodaikanal" (2,400), "vattakanal cottages" (480), "kodaikanal cottage booking" (390), "kodaikanal cottage stay" (320), all bedroom-size terms, and cottage price / rate / rent terms from this page.

## Key finding

The group-only keyword pool is tiny: about 400 searches a month in total, with a top term of 110. There are not 8 to 13 group keywords at high volume, so this page cannot be built from volume. It should win by being the clearest group-intent page (head count, event type, in-house catering, bonfire) while the home page carries the cottage terms.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Booking and travel listing sites: Booking.com (Pleasant Memories), MakeMyTrip (Sheila's Nest), Travelocity (Misty 7-Bedroom Hill Retreat).
- Property promotion pages: Spalba (Thapovan Farm Stay, Sterling Kodai Valley).
- Packages: WanderOn "Kodaikanal group trip from Bangalore, 1N 2D".
- Low quality: a Product Hunt post and Russian Laravel forum threads.

What they push:
- Whole-house options, e.g. 7 bedrooms and 9 bathrooms, from about $437 per night.
- Campfire, barbecue, large parking, homemade food for groups.
- Event capacity and in-house catering (Sterling Kodai Valley).
- Complete group trips with transport and a trip captain (WanderOn).

Your page against that:
- Already has H2s for featured group properties, booking, the area, tours for groups, reviews, planning and FAQ.
- Recommended H1 change: the current H1 ("Cottages in Kodaikanal for Group Stay, Under One Roof") leads with the home page's head term. Lead with the group phrase instead, e.g. "Group Stay in Kodaikanal, Under One Roof".
- Gap: no H2 built on group size (10 persons, 20 persons) or group type (friends, family reunion, corporate).
- Gap: group amenities (campfire, barbecue, parking, in-house food) are not in any heading.
- Gap: no H2 that targets "kodaikanal group accommodation" (110).
- Idea, unverified by keyword data: a group-trip-from-city angle (Bangalore, Chennai, Coimbatore) like WanderOn.

Cannibalization watch:
- Home page: owns every cottage head term (see scope rule above). Link from here to the home page rather than competing.
- `kodaikanal-group-tour-package.html` is a separate page and likely competes for "group" terms. Recommended: this page owns accommodation, that page owns the tour package.
- Villas page: owns "villas in kodaikanal for group stay".
- Homestay page: owns "homestay in kodaikanal for group".
- Dormitory page: owns "group stay refresh rooms dormitory".

Spam flag:
- Laravel forum threads promoting your name appeared again, plus a Product Hunt post with a near-identical title ("cottages-for-group-stay-in-kodaikanal"). Check Search Console backlinks. If you didn't create these, disavow and consider reporting them.

Caveat: group intent is a small term in this market. Expect this page to convert visitors who arrive from the home page, tours and ads, rather than drive much search traffic on its own.
