## Key finding

Package searching is small (3,600 for the head term), while the **places and sightseeing intent is about 40 times larger** (about 140,000 for the generic terms alone, and competition is rated Low, index 8 out of 100). Someone planning a trip searches for what to see before they search for a package. A tour package page that lists the places it covers, with names, can catch some of that traffic and turn it into booking enquiries.

For a hub page the sensible targets are the generic places terms ("tourist places", "places to visit", "things to do"). The individual attractions are better served by their own pages (see the table below).

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks for the package query:
- Large operators: [SOTC](https://www.sotc.in/india-tour-packages/kodaikanal-tour-packages), [Veena World](https://www.veenaworld.com/india/kodaikanal-tour-packages/ct), [MakeMyTrip holidays](https://www.makemytrip.com/holidays-india/kodaikanal-travel-packages.html).
- Marketplace: [Magpie Travel](https://magpie.travel/products/467ba110-1c9c-43ef-a2c9-d377d42171fe) (private 3-day tour with accommodation, and a student package).
- Weekend and blog operators: Muddietrails (2D1N from Bangalore), Expitours (Mysore-Ooty-Kodaikanal).
- No Kodaikanal-only local operator ranks.

What they push:
- Multi-day hill-station loops, mostly Ooty plus Kodaikanal, about ₹29,000 to ₹57,000.
- Short private trips with meals and instant confirmation.
- Customisation and luxury lines. Typical sights: Kodai Lake, Silver Cascade Falls, Pillar Rocks.

For the places intent I did not run a separate SERP check. The competition index (0 to 8) suggests the field is open, but the pages that rank are probably travel guides, not operators. Check the first page before committing to a guide page.

Your page against that:
- Strengths: clear price ("From ₹18,000 per couple"), stay and cab included, a package selector, local drivers, same-day response.
- Gap (volume): "Which Places Can You Cover?" does not carry "tourist places" or "places to visit" (49,500 + 14,800).
- Gap: no heading for family (140 + 140) or activities (880 + 320).
- Gap: "Where Can You Start Your Trip From?" has no city names in its heading.
- Gap: the title and H1 do not carry the exact "tour package in kodaikanal" wording.
- Easy renames:
  - "Which Places Can You Cover?" to "Kodaikanal Tourist Places Covered in Our Tour Packages"
  - "Where Can You Start Your Trip From?" to "Kodaikanal Tour Package from Bangalore, Chennai and Madurai"
  - Add "Kodaikanal Family Tour Package" and "Things to Do in Kodaikanal"
  - Add "Best Time to Visit Kodaikanal" (one short block)

Cannibalization watch:
- Couple file (same page): do not reuse the package, vacation packages or itinerary headings from that file.
- Day-count pages: own the 1, 2 and 3-day terms and "3 day itinerary" (210). Link to them from the package selector.
- Group Tour Package page: owns "group tour packages". Link to it.
- Route pages: keep one tour heading each.
- Travels page: owns "trip", "tour" and "agency" wording.
- Individual place pages (Berijam, park, village, valley): own their own attraction terms. The hub names the places and links to them.

Caveat: the places intent is huge but low-converting. Visitors from those searches need a clear path to a package (a price, a book button, and a link to the right day-count page).
