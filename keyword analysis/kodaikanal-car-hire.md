## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Expedia's Kodaikanal car hire page: a self-drive marketplace, so it does not match a with-driver offer.
- HurryUp Cabs: outstation pages with professional drivers, hatchback to tempo traveller, one-way and round-trip, Madurai airport transfers.
- CabBazar: outstation routes (Batlagundu, Tuticorin, Kodai Road), vehicles from compact hatchbacks to Innova Crysta, local hourly rental.

What they push:
- Route and distance pages (Bangalore about 447 km and 10 hours, Madurai about 114 km and 3 hours).
- One-way against round-trip pricing, vehicle ladder, airport transfers, hourly local rental.

Your page against that:
- The title ("Car Hire in Kodaikanal | Outstation & Multi-Day Hire with Driver") and the H1 ("Car Hire in Kodaikanal") match the primary and the page's angle well.
- The page has a "Plan your hire" widget (pick a length, preview your days), plus headings on what it is, cost, inclusions, where it can take you, families, best time, booking, reviews and FAQs.
- Gap (volume): no heading says "car for rent" (2,900), although the title and meta may cover it.
- Gap: no outstation heading for Bangalore, Chennai, Trichy or Rameshwaram (110 + 30 + 20 + 20).
- Gap: no "car booking" wording in a heading (50). "How Can You Book Car Hire in Kodaikanal?" is the closest.
- Gap: no mention of local hourly hire, which CabBazar offers. Decide whether that belongs here or on the Car Rental page.
- Easy renames:
  - "What Is Car Hire in Kodaikanal?" to "Car for Rent in Kodaikanal, With a Driver"
  - Add "Outstation Car Hire: Bangalore, Chennai and Beyond to Kodaikanal" as a new heading
  - "How Can You Book Car Hire in Kodaikanal?" to "Kodaikanal Car Booking: How It Works"
- Local detail is the opening: one driver for a multi-day trip, ghat-road experience, and the "Plan your hire" tool. The aggregators show no itinerary planning.

Cannibalization watch:
- Car Rental page: the biggest risk. Keep "rent a car" and "car rental" wording off this page, and keep "car hire" and "car for rent" wording off that page.
- Cabs and Call Taxi pages: keep "cab" and "taxi" wording off this page, except the Bangalore / Chennai / Rameshwaram route terms listed above.
- Route pages: link to them from "Where Can a Kodaikanal Car Hire Take You?".

Caveat: this page's value comes from two 2,900-volume head terms. Add a "car for rent" heading and an outstation section, and the rest of the pool adds only about 200 searches a month.
