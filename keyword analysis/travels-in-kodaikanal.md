## Key finding

"Travels in kodaikanal" is a mid-size term (1,300/mo), and the larger demand is for **packages** (3,600 each), which the tour packages hub owns. This page competes on the operator and planning wording instead: trip, tour, agency, planner, cost. The search results are travel blogs and guides rather than operators, so the intent is half "find a local travel company" and half "plan a trip". The page needs to serve both.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Travel blogs and guides: Revolving Compass, Muddie Trails, The Hosteller's travel checklist, Onmanorama, HIDMC, Mango Hill hotels blog.
- Aggregator guide: Expedia destination guide.
- Almost no local operator pages. The results are informational.

What they push:
- Getting there: Madurai airport about 120 km away, Kodai Road railway station about 80 km, Bangalore about 465 km (8 to 9 hours), a 2 to 3 hour taxi or bus from Kodai Road or Dindigul.
- Best time to visit by season (summer March to June, monsoon July to September, winter October to February).
- Sights (Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, Bryant Park, Kodai Lake, Bear Shola Falls), activities (Dolphin's Nose trek, cycling, horse riding), shopping and packing tips.

Your page against that:
- The H1 ("Travels in Kodaikanal") is clean and matches the primary.
- Current H2s are generic: "Build Your Kodaikanal Trip", "Kodaikanal at a Glance", "Featured Tours", "Plan Your Trip", "Three Steps to Your Kodaikanal Trip", "Where Are We Located?", "Common Questions About Travels in Kodaikanal".
- Gap (high volume): no H2 contains "trip" as a keyword phrase beyond "Build Your Kodaikanal Trip". The 1,300-volume "kodaikanal trip" is not an exact heading.
- Gap: no H2 for "travel agency" (390), "trip planner" (390), "best travels" (210) or "tour and travels" (210).
- Gap: no trip cost or price heading (90).
- Easy wins: rename "Plan Your Trip" to "Kodaikanal Trip Planner" and "Where Are We Located?" to something with "travel agency in Kodaikanal".
- The search results lean informational, so a short "getting to Kodaikanal" and "best time to visit" block would match intent. Keep it brief and link to the route pages for the details.

Cannibalization watch:
- Tour packages hub: owns the 3,600-volume package terms. Keep "package" wording out of this page's headings.
- Day-count tour pages and the couple tour page own their own count terms.
- Route pages own "X to kodaikanal" terms. Link to them, do not duplicate.
- Cab, taxi and car pages own the transport terms.

Caveat: "travels in kodaikanal" is a modest term, and packages are the larger prize. This page works best as the umbrella entry that sends visitors to the package and transport pages.
