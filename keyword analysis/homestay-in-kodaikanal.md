## Competitor analysis

Based on two search snapshots, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Aggregators: Booking.com, Cleartrip, eDreams, StayVista, Airbnb.
- Editorial: Outlook Traveller "best homestays in Kodaikanal" list.
- Individual properties: Maruti Villa, Radiant Hill, Happy Home Stay, Manimalar, Magizham, Whispering Waters.

Themes they push:
- Mountain or valley views.
- Distance to the lake (e.g. "1.6 km from Kodaikanal Lake").
- Farm-to-table or home-cooked meals.
- Garden, barbecue and fireplace.
- Family groups of up to 18.
- Per-night or per-person rates shown up front.

Your page against that:
- Already has H2s for family, mountain view, couples, budget, near lake, rates and reviews.
- Gap (high volume): no section for "vattakanal homestay" (390).
- Gap (high volume): nothing for "home stay & villa & cottage" (260).
- Gap (high volume): "home stay with food" (110) is only a sentence and needs its own H2.
- Gap (low volume): kitchen, pool, pet-friendly, campfire and self-cooking amenities appear nowhere. Add an amenities section or FAQ entries.
- The H1 ("A Kodaikanal Homestay, Run by a Family That Lives There") should lead with the exact 3,600-volume phrase "homestay in kodaikanal".

Caveat: aggregators will beat a single-property page on the generic terms. The best targets are the specific ones (family, view, food, price, Vattakanal), where the 100–400 volume sits.
