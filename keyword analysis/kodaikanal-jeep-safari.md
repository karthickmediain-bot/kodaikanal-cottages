## Key finding

The jeep safari pool is small: one large term ("kodaikanal jeep safari", 1,000) and one mid term ("jeep safari in kodaikanal", 260). The only other keyword above 100 is the broader "kodaikanal adventure activities" (320). Everything else is 90 or lower. Only two H2 keywords reach 100 (besides the primary), so I list the best eight available rather than padding.

The search results are thin on local competition: apart from two operator listings, the first page shows Kerala, Periyar, Munnar and Moab (US) safari pages and a Travelocity safari lodge page. A Kodaikanal-specific page can rank on the head terms with modest effort.

## Competitor analysis

Based on one search snapshot, not a full audit.

Who ranks:
- Operators: [GRT Great Trails](https://www.grthotels.com/great-trails-kodaikanal/great-kathas.html) (hotel safaris) and a "Travels Kodaikanal" about.me listing.
- Wrong destination: Kerala Tourism, Periyar Tourism, Munnar (Magpie), SOTC, Moab Jeep Safari (Wikipedia).
- Other: Travelocity safari lodges page.

What they push:
- GRT: a daytime off-road ride into Kombai Valley (9 AM to 2 PM, up to four guests, INR 10,000) and a night ride (7 PM, up to four guests, INR 6,500).
- The Travels Kodaikanal listing: Poombarai and Mannavanur village roads, waterfall trails, village rides, night safaris. No prices.
- Season guidance exists only for Kerala (October to March), not Kodaikanal.

Your page against that:
- The title ("Jeep Safari in Kodaikanal | Off-Road Trails & Viewpoints") and H1 ("Kodaikanal Jeep Safari") match the primary well.
- The page covers cost, which experience, what it is, places, best time, families, inclusions, booking, reviews, location and FAQs.
- Gap (volume): no heading uses "adventure activities" (320).
- Gap: no heading for night safari (30 + 30 + 20) or forest safari (50), if you offer them. The results show competitors offering night rides, so it may be worth adding if you run them.
- Gap: "safari in kodaikanal" and "kodaikanal safari" (40 + 50) have no heading of their own.
- Price is already the first heading ("How Much Does a Kodaikanal Jeep Safari Cost?"), which is strong: competitors show prices of INR 6,500 and INR 10,000, so state yours clearly near the top.
- Easy renames:
  - "Which Jeep Safari Experience Is Right for You?" to "Kodaikanal Adventure Activities: Pick Your Jeep Safari"
  - "What Places Can You Visit on a Kodaikanal Jeep Safari?" keep, and mention the village and forest trails by name
  - Add a heading for the night safari if it is offered
- Local detail is the opening: named trails and viewpoints, group size, duration, pickup point and season. The first page is mostly Kerala results.

Possible issue to check:
- The "Travels Kodaikanal" about.me listing shows the phone number +91 75023 45777, which is the number on your site. If you created it, it is a free extra listing and could be used as a backlink. If you did not, check who did. The URL spells it "travleskodaikanal".

Cannibalization watch:
- Tent Stay page: owns "tent stay jeep ride". Do not repeat it here.
- Tour packages hub: owns sightseeing package and tour terms. Link to it from the "places" section.
- Cabs and Call Taxi pages: keep "cab" and "taxi" wording off this page.

Caveat: the jeep safari niche is small apart from the head term (1,000). Keep the page focused on the product, price and trails, and treat "adventure activities" (320) as a second target if you add a short adventure overview.
