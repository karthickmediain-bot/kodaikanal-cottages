## Scope rule: how this page uses transport keywords

You do not sell bus, train or flight tickets, so those keywords are **bridge keywords**: answer the question briefly and honestly, then show the cab as the easier way to make the last leg. Rules (same as `coimbatore-to-kodaikanal.md`):

- Never promise bus, train or flight bookings.
- Give third-party figures (fares, timings, train frequency) as "about" and check them before publishing.
- End every mode block with a cab offer: pickup from the airport, station or bus stand straight to the stay.
- This page summarises each city route and links to the route page. It does not try to out-rank those pages.
