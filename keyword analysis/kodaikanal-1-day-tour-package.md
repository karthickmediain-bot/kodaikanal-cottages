Page: Kodaikanal 1 day tour package (one-day sightseeing package page)

Status: draft, awaiting approval.
Source: Google Ads Keyword Planner (India), monthly average searches (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026). Runs: keyword ideas from 4 seeds (75 ideas) and a 25-phrase check of price, origin-city, cab, itinerary and place phrases.
Brand terms excluded. Some phrases returned no data, which means Keyword Planner has no volume for them, not that they were skipped.

## Key finding

The exact phrase "kodaikanal 1 day tour package" has **no volume** in Keyword Planner. The whole "1 day package" family is tiny (20 to 90 a month). The real demand sits in three places:

1. **"1 day" places and itinerary searches** (about 1,800 across phrasings) from people planning what to see.
2. **Sightseeing cab / local sightseeing** (390 for "kodaikanal sightseeing cab"), the closest high-intent match for what a 1-day package actually is: a cab plus a fixed route.
3. **Generic places lists** (2,900 for "sightseeing places list") that a package page can catch by naming every stop.

Competition is Low (index 0 to 26) on almost every term, so this page can rank for the long tail quickly.

Do not target "holiday packages for kodaikanal" (3,600). That belongs to the tour packages hub, not a 1-day page.

The origin-city "package from" terms (Bangalore 720, Chennai 590) are real demand but they belong to multi-day trips, since nobody travels from Bangalore for a single day. Cover them on the 2 day and 3 day pages and link to those from the start-points block here.

### Recommended changes to the structure above

1. **Add a price table** (sedan / SUV / tempo traveller, per vehicle) and keep "price" and "cost" wording in the paragraph above it.
2. **Add a "What is not included" H3** (entry fees, jeep safari, boating, meals, tips).
3. **Add a time column** to the itinerary (start time, stops, end time).
4. **Add a short terms block:** advance, payment, cancellation, customisation.
5. **Add one trust line:** "Kodaikanal travel agency / tour operator" wording, plus reviews if available.
6. **Add one cross-link:** the Ooty combined package.
7. **Add three FAQs:** "What is not included?", "How much advance do I pay?", "What is the cancellation policy?"

The competitor gap is clear: few operators publish a real 1 day itinerary with a fixed route and a vehicle price. A page that states the route, hours and price per vehicle serves a need the search results do not.
