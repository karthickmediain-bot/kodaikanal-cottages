About Kodaikanal Trip Packages

It is located in the Dindigul district of Tamil Nadu, India. This place is well-known for its incredible natural beauty, pleasant climate, and serene environment. That's one of the major reasons most tourists visit Kodaikanal every year. Kodaikanal has popular tourist spots such as Kodai Lake, Coaker's Walk, Bear Shola Falls, Pillar Rocks, Dolphin's Nose, and Guna Caves.

If you're planning a vacation, then choosing a Kodaikanal trip package from Cottages Kodaikanal is the best option for you. Based on your travel preference, the type of experience, and the duration of your stay, we have tour packages. Here are the types of Kodai trip packages that we provide:

It is necessary to book a tour package in advance with Cottages Kodaikanal. In peak season and special occasions, tour packages are high in demand. So you have to book your tour package early. Therefore, you can avoid last-minute hassles.

02

Check the season

It is always good to consider climatic conditions before a tour package. Our tour operator will guide you through the best season from April to June. You can choose your preferred date and time for your vacation.

03

Carry valid ID

As a tourist, you should carry a valid ID proof copy of your Aadhar card. If you are married, then it is mandatory to carry a registered marriage certificate.
