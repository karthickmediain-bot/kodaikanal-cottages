Sightseeing

Places to Visit on a Kodaikanal Trip

One cab can cover several stops in a day, because most tourist places in Kodaikanal sit close to the town centre. Treat this as your sightseeing list and work down from the top. If you only have a day, the first four are the must visit places in Kodaikanal. Our full guide adds distances and timings.

01

Kodai Lake

The star-shaped lake at the heart of town. Go boating, rent a cycle or walk the loop.

02

Coaker's Walk

A cliff-edge path with wide valley views. Go early, before the mist rolls in.

03

Pillar Rocks

Three granite pillars over 100 m high above a deep valley.

04

Bryant Park

A botanical garden with flower beds and seasonal blooms. Check Bryant Park timings on the day.

05

Silver Cascade and Bear Shola Falls

Two easy waterfall stops. See waterfalls in Kodaikanal.

06

Guna Caves

Deep rock chambers in pine forest, made famous by a Tamil film.

Viewpoints

Dolphin's Nose, Coaker's Walk and Other Viewpoints

Past the busy lake, the edges of town hold the hidden places in Kodaikanal that photographs never quite capture. Mist, cliff edges and deep valleys decide the view, so plan viewpoints for the morning.

Short trek

Dolphin's Nose

A rock overhang above a deep valley, about 8 km from town and reached on a short, moderate trek. Go in clear weather, wear grip shoes and check conditions on the day.

Easy walk

Coaker's Walk

A paved cliff-edge path of about 1 km with telescope views. Early morning gives the clearest sight of the plains.

Sunrise

Green Valley View and Sunrise Points

Pillar Rocks and the green valley view point are classic stops. For the best sunrise point in Kodaikanal, be at the rail before first light.

Season

Best Time for a Kodaikanal Trip

The best season runs from April to June, when skies are clear and the hills are green. It is also the busiest stretch, so book early. If you are working out the best time to visit Kodaikanal with family, aim for the same months, when children can walk, boat and play outside comfortably. Getting there

How to Reach Kodaikanal

Travelling to Kodaikanal is straightforward, but the hill town has no railway station of its own. Reach a nearby station, airport or town, then finish the climb by road.

120

By air: Madurai

65

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best month to visit Kodaikanal?

April to June is the best time to visit Kodaikanal, with clear skies, green hills and mild sun. Monsoon brings mist and full waterfalls, while winter turns chilly and foggy. Families usually prefer the summer months.

How many days are enough for a Kodaikanal trip?

Two days is enough for a relaxed Kodaikanal trip plan covering the lake, viewpoints and falls. One day shows you the highlights, and three days lets you add forests, caves and a village walk without rushing.

How much does a Kodaikanal trip cost?

Prices depend on the season, group size, stay type and number of days. Share your dates with Cottages Kodaikanal on +91 75023 45777, and we will quote a package with no hidden extras.

What are the must-visit places in Kodaikanal?

The must visit places in Kodaikanal are Kodai Lake, Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, Dolphin's Nose, Bryant Park, Silver Cascade and Bear Shola Falls. On a longer visit, add Guna Caves and the Berijam forest.

Is Kodaikanal good in the rainy season?

The rainy season in Kodaikanal suits travellers who enjoy mist, green valleys and full waterfalls. Expect heavy showers and slower roads, so keep a flexible plan and book a stay with a warm, dry common area.

What are the Dolphin's Nose timings?

Dolphin's Nose is best visited in daylight and clear weather, and conditions change with the season. Start early, wear grip shoes, and our driver will check the weather before leaving so we can adjust your day.

Which places to visit in Kodaikanal with family are best?

For families, the best picks are boating at Kodai Lake, the flower beds at Bryant Park, the easy Coaker's Walk and the waterfalls. Pillar Rocks works for older children, and we pace each day around the youngest traveller.
