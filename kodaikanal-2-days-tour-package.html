Kodaikanal 2 Days Itinerary, Day by Day

Best for couples, families and small groups visiting for a weekend or for the first time, this Kodaikanal trip plan for 2 days has two different moods. Day one stays close to the lake. Day two heads out to the forest and the falls. A night in between means nobody is rushing.

    Arrival & pickup

    Hotel check-in

    Kodaikanal Lake & Bryant Park

    Boat on the star-shaped lake, then stroll through Bryant Park nearby.
