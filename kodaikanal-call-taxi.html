How it works

What Is a Kodaikanal Call Taxi Service?

A call taxi is an on-demand car you phone for, with no full-day package needed. It suits visitors, families and groups on short, specific trips inside and around the town, not a fixed sightseeing loop.

Guests usually ring for a lake-to-hotel hop, a quick run to Coaker's Walk before dinner, or a bus stand pickup at short notice. Based on our experience of 12 years on these town routes, our vetted local drivers stay on call, so a ride arranged on the spot feels as safe as one booked days ahead.

Travelling with family

Is a Kodaikanal Taxi Safe for Families?

  • A sedan or SUV is available for families with young children or elders who need an easier step in
  • Every vehicle has fitted seatbelts, which matters on the winding ghat roads that circle the town
  • Your driver is local and knows the easiest drop points near hotels, homestays and cottages in town
  • Waits between stops stay short on multi-stop bookings, so tired children are not kept in the car

Good to know: for late-night bus stand pickups, call at least 30 minutes ahead so we can confirm a driver. Carrying a lot of luggage? Ask for an SUV instead of a hatchback, and mention any elders or young children when you book so we send the right car for everyone travelling with you.

Timing

What Is the Best Time to Use a Call Taxi in Kodaikanal?

Mar – May

Summer

Holiday traffic peaks across town, so calling 20 to 30 minutes ahead helps you secure a car at the busiest hours of the day, even on a busy long weekend.

Jun – Sep

Monsoon

Fewer tourists mean quicker pickups on most days, though heavy rain can slow a car's arrival by a few extra minutes on the hill roads, which we factor in.

Oct – Nov

Autumn

The quietest window of the year, with the shortest turnaround, the easiest on-demand pickups and the very calmest roads around the lake and the market.

Dec – Feb

Winter

The busiest season in town, so a short advance heads-up helps you get a car at the peak morning and evening hours, especially around the Christmas week.
