When Is the Best Time to Get the Cheapest Car Rental in Kodaikanal?

Mar – May

Summer

Peak demand for local and outstation cars fills the calendar quickly in these warm weeks, so reserve a vehicle three to four days before you travel, especially for weekends.

Jun – Sep

Monsoon

Quieter roads and easy same-day availability make planning simple in these green weeks, though ghat stretches move slowly after heavy rain showers. Allow extra travel time.

Oct – Nov

Autumn

A calm shoulder season brings good availability, steady rates and clear views across the hills, with very little pressure on last-minute bookings. Ideal for relaxed days.

Dec – Feb

Winter

The second peak around Christmas and New Year brings the highest demand and rates of the year, so book your car early for these cold, crisp weeks. Fleets fill up fast.
