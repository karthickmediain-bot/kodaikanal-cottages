How it works

What Is a Taxi Service in Kodaikanal?

A Kodaikanal taxi service is on-demand, point-to-point transport within and around the town. You call, we send a car, and you get where you're going without booking a full-day package. It's built for short, specific trips rather than a fixed sightseeing loop.

Guests usually call for a lake-to-hotel hop, a quick run to Coaker's Walk before dinner, or a bus stand pickup at short notice. We keep a pool of vetted local drivers on call, so a short call-in ride is just as safe and comfortable as a pre-booked one.
