The tour

Why Choose a Kodaikanal Valley Tour?

The Kodaikanal Valley is a treasure trove of natural beauty, and the valley tour is designed to showcase the essence of this stunning hill station. Whether you're looking to capture Instagram-worthy landscapes, explore hidden gems, or simply bask in the beauty of nature, the Kodaikanal Valley Tour is your gateway to an unforgettable experience.

Best Time for the Kodaikanal Valley Tour

  • Summer (March to June): The weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing.
  • Monsoon (June to September): Adds a mystical charm to the valley, with lush greenery and waterfalls in full flow.
  • October to February: Quieter and cooler, with crisp air and clear skies.

Things to Carry for the Kodaikanal Valley Tour

  • Comfortable Footwear: For walking and trekking through various valleys and trails.
  • Camera: To capture the breathtaking views and wildlife encounters.
  • Sunscreen: Even though Kodaikanal is cool, the sun can still be strong.
  • Light Jacket or Sweater: It can get chilly, especially in the evenings.
  • Binoculars: For birdwatching and spotting wildlife.

The route

Valley Tour Route

A single loop through Vattakanal's terraced slopes and the cliffside viewpoints above it, with a waterfall stop on the way back.

Valley Loop · Vattakanal & Viewpoints

4 Stops
  1. 1
    08:00 AM

    Pickup

    Our driver picks you up from your hotel, bus stand or railway station and heads toward Vattakanal.

  2. 2
    08:45 AM

    Vattakanal

    Terraced slopes and forest views over the valley — the anchor stop of the whole route.

  3. 3
    10:00 AM

    Dolphin's Nose

    A narrow rock outcrop with a sheer drop and an open view across the valley floor.

  4. 4
    11:15 AM

    Echo Point (Green Valley View)

    A wide cliffside lookout, one of the more photographed points on the loop.

  5. 5
    12:15 PM

    Silver Cascade Falls

    A roadside waterfall stop on the way back toward town — the full-day route adds extra time here.

  6. 6
    01:30 PM

    Drop-off

    Return to your pickup point, or continue on to your next stop on the full-day route.
