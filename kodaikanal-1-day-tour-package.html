The Route

Your day in Kodaikanal, hour by hour

Nine stops in a fixed order, timed around travel and opening hours so you spend the day at the sights and not in the car.

Day 1 · Arrival & sightseeing

9 stops
  1. 07:30 AM

    Pickup & hotel check-in

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, bus stand or railway station. Check in and freshen up first.

  2. 08:30 AM

    Kodaikanal Lake & Rose Garden

    Boat on the star-shaped lake, then walk through Rose Garden next door.

  3. 09:30 AM

    Bryant Park

    A short stop at the botanical garden beside the lake.

  4. 10:15 AM

    Coaker’s Walk, Upper Lake View & Green Valley View

    A cliffside path linking three of the best panoramic viewpoints.

  5. 11:30 AM

    Pillar Rocks, Pine Forest & Guna Cave

    Three granite pillars, a shaded pine trail and the cave made famous by the film “Guna.”

  6. 01:00 PM

    Lunch break On your own

    A relaxed stop for lunch. It is not part of the package.

  7. 02:30 PM

    Moir Point & Pambar Falls

    A quieter ridge-top viewpoint, then a roadside waterfall.

  8. 04:30 PM

    Free time near the lake

    Shop, grab a coffee or simply rest before heading back.

  9. 07:30 PM

    Dinner & overnight stay Included

    Back at the hotel for dinner and a night’s rest.

Day 2 · Departure morning

Checkout
  1. 07:30 AM

    Breakfast Included

    Breakfast at the hotel before checkout.

  2. 09:00 AM

    Checkout

    Pack up and settle out of your room.

  3. 09:30 AM

    Drop-off

    Dropped at your pickup point: hotel, bus stand or railway station.

We confirm the exact order against travel time, weather and attraction timings.

Questions

Before you book

What does the 1 Day / 1 Night package include?

One night’s stay, a private cab for pickup, sightseeing and drop-off, plus breakfast and dinner.

Are lunch and entrance tickets included?

No. Lunch and attraction entrance tickets are paid on the day unless your confirmed package says otherwise.

Can I be picked up from Madurai, Coimbatore or Palani?

Yes. Pickup is available from Coimbatore, Madurai, Dindigul, Palani and Kodai Road, and from your hotel, bus stand or railway station in Kodaikanal. Mention your pickup point when you enquire.

Is the cab private?

Yes. The package is built around a private cab. Confirm the vehicle type and seating capacity when you book.

Can the route be changed?

Yes, where the confirmed terms allow it. The order can also shift with weather, traffic and attraction timings.

How fast will I get a price?

We check real availability for your dates and group size, and usually reply within a few hours.

Is one day enough for Kodaikanal?

It covers the main sights: the lake, viewpoints, pine forest and a waterfall. If you want a slower pace or the farther spots, look at the 2 or 3 day packages.
